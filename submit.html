Submit a manuscript

Two ways in. If you have an account, use the form — it keeps your submission history in one place. Otherwise, email the editorial office.

Before you submit

  • The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.
  • All authors have agreed to the submission and to open review.
  • You are willing to deposit the preprint on Zenodo (we will walk you through it).

Papers are published under CC BY 4.0 unless stated otherwise.

Submitting by email

Send the manuscript as a PDF attachment, with a short covering message giving the title, authors, affiliations and a contact address.
