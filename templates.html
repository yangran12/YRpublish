Manuscript templates

Use whichever format you already work in. All three produce the same PDF — we do not require a particular one.

Word (.docx)

The usual choice. Open it, type over the placeholder text, export to PDF. WPS opens it too.

Typst (.typ)

Nothing to install — open typst.app in a browser, paste the file, export a PDF. The best middle ground if LaTeX feels heavy.

What to send — Send the PDF. Keep the source file (the .docx or .tex) — you will need it if the paper comes back for revision.

Before you submit

  • Title, all authors and all affiliations are complete and in the right order.
  • Abstract is 150–250 words, with 3–6 keywords.
  • Every figure and table is numbered, captioned, and referred to in the text.
  • References are complete and in a consistent style.
  • The work is not under consideration elsewhere.
  • All authors have agreed to submission and to open review.
