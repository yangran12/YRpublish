About TDF

TDF is an open review platform run from Nanjing Normal University. It is not a registered journal and does not claim to be one.

What TDF is, and is not

It is

  • An organising body for open peer review.
  • A public archive of review records.
  • A citable index of accepted preprints.
  • An editorial team at Nanjing Normal University, advised by faculty.

It is not

  • A journal with an ISSN or CN number.
  • Indexed in Web of Science or Scopus.
  • A substitute for peer-reviewed publication elsewhere.
  • A venue that charges authors or readers.

Note for authors and readers — TDF has no ISSN or CN number and is not indexed in Web of Science or Scopus. A TDF listing is not a journal publication and should not be presented as one on a CV, in a degree application, or in a funding report.

Publication ethics

TDF follows the COPE core practices on authorship, conflicts of interest, data integrity and handling of misconduct. In outline:

  • Authorship is limited to those who made a substantive contribution. Gift and ghost authorship are not accepted.
  • Plagiarism, data fabrication and duplicate submission lead to rejection, and the decision is recorded publicly.
  • Conflicts of interest must be declared by authors and reviewers alike.
  • Reviewers may not use unpublished manuscripts for their own work.
  • Complaints may be sent to the editorial office; we will respond in writing.
