How it works

Four steps, no publication charges, and a public record at every stage.

You deposit the preprint

Upload your manuscript to Zenodo. It receives a DOI immediately — it is citable from day one, while review is still running.

We assign reviewers

Two reviewers are invited. Their reports are posted openly, with the manuscript and your responses, in a public review thread.

You revise

Revisions are handled in the same public thread, so the history of the paper is legible to anyone who reads it later.

We index it

Accepted papers get a TDF number and appear in the index here, linked to their DOI. Authors may still submit to a journal elsewhere.

Scope

We consider work in the following areas. Interdisciplinary submissions are welcome.

Electric mobility

Vehicle-grid integration, charging infrastructure, powertrain electrification, fleet operation.

Renewable energy

Solar, wind, storage, hydrogen, and the system-level problems that come with scaling them.

Smart grids

Dispatch, stability, demand response, market design, distribution-level operation.

AI in energy systems

Forecasting, control, optimisation and data-driven methods applied to the grid.

Submit a manuscript

There is no submission fee and no article processing charge. If you are unsure whether your work fits, write to us before submitting.
